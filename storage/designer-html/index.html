Official Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please read the official voting rules and participation guidelines for Dibrugarh Durgo Utsav 2026.

  • 1. One Vote Per Category: Each voter is entitled to cast only 1 vote per award category (Best Puja, Best Pratima, Best Lighting) during the active voting period.
  • 2. Fair Play & Anti-Spam Protection: All votes are verified in real time. Automated bot scripts, IP manipulation, or duplicate spam votes will be automatically detected and purged.
  • 3. Nominated Committee Eligibility: Only officially registered and shortlisted Sarbajanin Durga Puja committees within Dibrugarh municipality boundaries are eligible for voting.
  • 4. Jury & Public Weighted Score: Winners of Dibrugarh Sharad Samman 2026 are determined by a weighted combination of verified public voting and independent jury panel evaluations.
  • 5. Safety & Environmental Compliance: Participating committees must strictly adhere to eco-friendly idol guidelines, fire safety, noise limiters, and crowd management standards.
  • 6. Voting Timeline Adherence: Online voting opens on 20th September 2026 and closes on 30th September 2026 at midnight. Votes received after the cutoff will not be accepted.
  • 7. Final Decision Authority: The final decision of the Dibrugarh Sharad Samman 2026 Organising Committee and Jury Board is binding and non-contestable.
